Corporate Compliance Training

Modernized the compliance curriculum delivered annually to 5,000+ employees, maintained a 100% pass rate on all regulatory and internal audits over 6 years (including audits tied to Centene's $2.2B acquisition), and reduced 1:1 compliance support by over 80%.

My role

Manager, Training

Audience

Federal contracting staff

Tools

Articulate Storyline, Rise, Audacity, Vyond, Synthesia

Timeline

8 weeks

The challenge

For years, Magellan's compliance curriculum, covering Code of Conduct, Fraud, Waste, and Abuse, and Government Contracts, HIPAA Privacy, and cybersecurity requirements, consisted of simple click-next PowerPoint slides. Learners clicked through without engaging, and there was no reliable way to confirm the content had actually landed.

My process

01

Needs analysis

Interviewed subject matter experts, policy writers, and attorneys to map where real errors happened, then wrote objectives against those moments — not the regulation table of contents.

02

Storyboarding

Designed branching decision scenarios drawn from actual case files, with consequences that played out the way they would in the real health care industry.

03

Build & iterate

Built in various authoring tools with custom feedback layers, then ran two rounds of pilot testing with SMEs to tune difficulty and remove ambiguity.

The solution

Recommended and led the shift to interactive, scenario-based learning across the full compliance curriculum. Built each course individually in various authoring tools with branching interactivity, animated video, knowledge checks, and narration, translating dense regulatory requirements into scenarios employees could actually apply.
